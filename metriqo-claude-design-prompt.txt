## 1. Product summary

- **What it is:** professional invoicing for small businesses (about 1–20 people) in the US, UK and Europe, built for non-accountants. Create invoices, track customers and payments, share invoice PDFs, and share a digital business card.
- **Offline-first:** all data lives in a SQLite database on the phone. The app is fully usable with no internet.
- **No user account or sign-in.** The business profile is the identity. All data stays on the device unless the user backs it up.
- **It is not** a POS, inventory, accounting ledger, expense tracker, CRM or payment processor. Don't design stock, ledgers, card payments or online payment collection.
- **Platform:** Android (Google Play). Portrait only. Light mode only today. Phones first.
- **Tech stack (for handoff):** React Native 0.86 + Expo SDK 57 (development client / EAS builds), TypeScript, React Navigation 7 (native stack), Zustand state, React Hook Form + Zod validation, Drizzle ORM + expo-sqlite, expo-print (HTML → PDF) + pdf-lib (page numbers), expo-sharing, expo-mail-composer, react-native-qrcode-svg, react-native-svg, @expo/vector-icons (the **Feather** icon set is used everywhere), react-native-safe-area-context, RevenueCat (react-native-purchases + react-native-purchases-ui) with Google Play Billing, expo-auth-session (Google sign-in for Drive), expo-local-authentication (app lock), @react-native-community/datetimepicker. No UI kit library: styles are React Native `StyleSheet` objects that read one token file. The system font is used; no custom font is loaded.

## 5. Core business rules your designs must express

### 5.1 One pricing method per invoice
- Every invoice has **exactly one Pricing Method**, and **all its lines use that method**. There are nine methods: General, Quantity, Weight, Length, Area, Volume, Time, Service, Custom.
- The business's default method (set on the Invoice Type screen or in Invoice Settings) pre-fills new invoices.
- On the Invoice Items screen the method is a dropdown:
  - With no lines, it can be changed freely.
  - With lines, a confirmation dialog appears. Switching within the same family (General ↔ Quantity ↔ Service) keeps the lines ("Changing the pricing method will affect all existing invoice items. Do you want to continue?"). Any other switch **removes all lines** and uses a destructive confirmation ("…isn't compatible with the items already on this invoice — they'll be removed…").
- When **editing** an existing invoice, the method is read-only. A **duplicate** can change it.
- Each catalog item also has one pricing method. The item picker shows only items with the invoice's method. Tapping an incompatible item shows: "Incompatible pricing method — This item uses X pricing and cannot be added to this Y invoice." Creating a new item from the picker preselects the invoice's method.
- Every pricing method is available on every subscription plan.

### 5.2 Fields and units by method

| Method | Measurement input(s) | Unit choices | Price label | Billable quantity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| General | Quantity | Unit: pcs, unit, box, pack, set, dozen, hr, kg, ltr, m | Unit Price | quantity |
| Quantity | Quantity | same as General | Unit Price | quantity |
| Service | Quantity | same as General | "Service price" (name label: "Service name") | quantity |
| Weight | Weight | kg, g, lb, oz | Price per weight unit | weight |
| Length | Length | m, cm, mm, ft, in, yd | Price per length unit | length |
| Area | Length, Width (one shared unit) | m, cm, mm, ft, in, yd | Price per area unit | length × width (shown as "m²") |
| Volume | Length, Width, Height (one shared unit) | m, cm, mm, ft, in, yd | Price per volume unit | L × W × H (shown as "m³") |
| Time | Duration | minute, hr, day (8 hr), week (40 hr) | Rate per time unit | duration |
| Custom | Business picks from: description, SKU, quantity, unit, weight, length, width, height, discount, tax | generic units | Unit Price | **quantity only** (other measurements are informational) |

- Every line has Item name (required) and a price (required). Description, SKU (not on Time), Discount % and Tax % (0–100) appear depending on the method.
- Every unit dropdown ends with "+ Add custom unit". Custom units are saved for the business, per unit family.
- Changing a unit **does not convert** the number. The price is always per the unit entered.
- The method's measurement must be greater than 0. Quantity, when present, must be greater than 0.
- The line editor groups fields into cards: Pricing Method (read-only, or a dropdown only when this is the first line), Line Details (name, description, SKU), Quantity & Unit, Pricing, Logistics & Dimensions, and a live Line Total Preview.

### 5.3 Unit Price vs Total Item Price (per line)
- Each invoice line and each catalog item has a two-option toggle: **Unit Price | Total Item Price**. This is separate from the invoice's pricing method, and lines on one invoice can use different modes.
- **Unit Price:** line subtotal = billable quantity × unit price.
- **Total Item Price:** the amount entered **is** the line subtotal and is **never multiplied** by the quantity. Measurements are still required and still shown for information (for example "20 m²").
- Both values stay in the form while the user switches modes.
- Discount % is taken off first, then Tax % is applied to the discounted amount. Each line is rounded to the cent.
- The live "Line Total Preview" shows: the calculated quantity with its unit; "× [price] unit price" or "Total Item Price (not multiplied by quantity)"; any discount; "+[tax] VAT" or "No VAT"; and the **Line Total**.
- A catalog item's mode pre-fills the line's mode.
- On the PDF, a Total Item Price line shows "—" in the Unit price column.
- Line rows in lists show the item name, the measurement text (for example "5m × 4m (20 m²)"), the price or mode, and the line total.

### 5.4 Totals
- Invoice totals are Subtotal, Discount, Tax and Grand total. (The app says "VAT" in some places and "Tax" in others; please standardise.)
- There is **no** invoice-level discount, shipping or fee. Discount and tax are per line only.
- Line amounts are frozen when the invoice is saved. Later changes to catalog items never change old invoices.

### 5.5 Status = payment status only
- Status is calculated, never set by hand:
  - **Paid:** paid ≥ total, and total > 0.
  - otherwise **Overdue:** the due date has passed.
  - otherwise **Partial:** some amount has been paid.
  - otherwise **Unpaid**.
- There is **no workflow status**: no Draft, Sent, Viewed or Void. Pressing Save on the Review screen creates the real, numbered invoice immediately. (The current Review screen wrongly shows a "DRAFT" pill and a fake "Save as Draft" button. Remove both.)
- Invoice number: automatic, in the format prefix + business code + "-" + sequence (for example `INV-<code>-12`). The "next number" preview is shown on Review and the number is reserved on save. It cannot be edited on the invoice. The prefix can be edited in settings; the next sequence number is read-only.
- Status filter options: All, Unpaid, Partial, Paid, Overdue.

### 5.6 Payments
- A payment always belongs to one invoice. Fields:
  - Amount Received: required, greater than 0, and **no more than the remaining balance** ("Amount can't exceed the remaining balance (X).").
  - Payment Date: required, not before the invoice issue date.
  - Method: Cash, Bank transfer, Card, PayPal or Other.
  - Reference / Transaction ID: optional.
  - Internal Notes: optional.
- Invoice money summary: Total invoiced, Amount paid, Remaining (or **Overpaid**, if an edit lowered the total below what was already paid), percentage settled with a progress bar, and the list of payments.
- "Record Payment" is disabled when nothing is left to pay.
- Edit Payment lets the user change any field or delete the payment.
- Payment History lists every payment, with search and a method filter.
- Customer balances (total invoiced, paid, outstanding, overdue, invoice count) are always calculated.
- Deleting an invoice **also deletes its payments**. The current confirmation doesn't say this; please include it in the copy.

### 5.7 Subscription plans (RevenueCat + Google Play)

| Plan | Invoices per calendar month | Older invoices | Customer history | Fallback display price (monthly / yearly) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free | 5 | Only for 24 hours after each invoice is created | Locked | — |
| Starter "For freelancers" | 15 | Yes | Yes | $5 / $48 |
| Business "Growing operations" — badge "MOST POPULAR" | 40 | Yes | Yes | $10 / $96 |
| Pro "Established teams" | 100 | Yes | Yes | $15 / $144 |
| Unlimited "Maximum scale" | Unlimited | Yes | Yes | $20 / $192 |

Plan card features are generated from rules: "N invoices / month" or "Unlimited invoices", "Historical invoices" or "Recent invoices only (24 hours)", "Historical customers" or "Customer history locked", and "Works offline".

- **Prices:** the localized Google Play price appears once it has loaded. The USD prices above are shown only when the store can't be reached. A yearly saving ("Save X%") is shown only when the real store prices make yearly cheaper.
- **Allowance:** monthly and yearly versions of a plan have the same monthly allowance. It **resets on the 1st of each calendar month** in local time. Deleting an invoice does **not** give the slot back. Editing an invoice doesn't use a slot; duplicating one does.
- **Usage meter** (on the Dashboard and the Pricing screen): "X of Y invoices" with a bar and "Resets 1 Nov", or "Unlimited invoices".
- **Limit reached:**
  - Tapping New Invoice or Duplicate at the limit opens a modal: "Monthly invoice limit reached", the plan name, "X of Y used", "Your plan includes N invoices a month. Upgrade to create more — your existing invoices are safe.", with buttons **View Plans** and **Not now**.
  - The same check runs again on Save. The modal appears there too, and the work in progress is kept.
- **Free 24-hour lock:** an invoice older than 24 hours opens as a full-screen locked view (lock icon, "Historical invoices are locked", invoice number and created date, "Free plans can open an invoice for 24 hours after it is created. Upgrade to open older invoices — nothing has been deleted.", with **View plans** and **Go back**). This covers Invoice Detail, Edit Invoice, the PDF screen, Record Payment and Edit Payment. In lists, these rows show a **Locked** badge.
- **Customer History** is locked on Free. A modal says "Customer history is locked" with the customer name and "Basic customer info stays available — nothing is locked or removed." Customer contacts themselves are never locked.
- **Pricing screen** ("Plans & Subscription"):
  - An optional reason banner (invoice limit, locked invoice or locked customer) with an **Upgrade now** button.
  - A "Verify your subscription" banner when the plan is stale.
  - A Current Plan card: plan name, status pill, renewal or end date line, offline note, usage meter, plan features, Change Plan, **Manage Subscription** (only for an active Google Play subscription), Restore Purchases and Customer Center.
  - A Monthly / Yearly toggle.
  - Plan cards with these CTAs:
    - "Upgrade to X", "Switch to X", "Current plan" (disabled) or "Working…".
    - For a downgrade: "Starts at your next renewal — you keep your current plan until then."
    - On Free, for a paid user: "Switch to Free", with "Cancel your subscription in Google Play. You keep your paid plan until the end of the billing period." Once cancelled: "Your X plan ends <date>. Metriqo moves to Free automatically after that."
  - A store error card with Retry.
  - Fine print: Google Play handles billing, and "Your invoices, customers and payments always stay saved on this device."
- **Statuses to design:** Free, Active ("Renews <date>"), Cancelled but active ("Access ends <date> — it will not renew"), Expired, Billing issue, Pending, Unknown, Offline, Restoring, Loading.
- **Offline:** a paid plan last confirmed by RevenueCat keeps working offline for up to **3 days** after that check. After that the app applies Free rules (so older invoices look locked) until it reconnects. Purchases, restores and plan changes need internet. Moving to Free never deletes data.

### 5.8 What works offline
- **Fully offline:** dashboard; invoices (create, edit, duplicate, delete); customers; items; payments; PDF generation and templates; QR code; business card; app lock.
- **Needs internet:**
  - Google Drive sign-in, backup and restore.
  - Cloud backup.
  - Subscription purchase, restore, verification and Customer Center.
  - Actually sending something through WhatsApp, email or the share sheet.
  - Opening website and map links.
- Today there is **no global offline indicator**. Offline state appears only on the Pricing screen and in backup errors ("You're offline — connect to the internet to back up or restore."). Design offline states for those surfaces. A small global offline indicator is optional and NEW.

### 5.9 Backup and restore (Google Drive)
- **Sign in with Google / Sign out.** Sign-out confirmation: "Sign out of Google Drive? You can sign back in any time — your existing backups stay on Drive."
- **Backup now:** shows "Backing up…" while running, then an alert: "Backup complete" or "Backup failed" with the reason.
- **Automatic Daily Backup** toggle. It runs at most about once a day, and only when the app is opened or brought to the foreground while signed in. It is not a true background job, so don't promise backups while the app is closed.
- **Last Successful Backup** card: time, archive size, and counts (invoices, customers, items). A "Last attempt" row shows the status.
- The 10 most recent Drive backups are kept.
- **Backup History:** a summary (number of backups, total size), the list of Drive backups each with **Restore**, an attempt log with filter chips (All / Automatic / Manual), **Retry** on failed backups, and a "Create Manual Backup Now" button.
- **Restore confirmation** (one destructive dialog): "Restore this backup? This replaces all data currently on this device with the backup from <date>. This cannot be undone." with Cancel and Restore. The file is checked before anything changes, and the restore is all-or-nothing. If it fails: "Restore failed — Your existing data was not changed." On success: "Restore complete." **No safety copy of the current data is taken before restoring.**
- **Cloud Backup** (optional paid add-on) screens exist: an enable toggle, storage used vs limit (Free 250 MB; Plus 5 GB marked "Coming soon"), status, Backup now, Cloud Backup History with restore, and an Upgrade Storage plan picker. **The cloud server is not deployed**, so today these screens show a "not configured" error. Redesign them, but label them "backend not live". Keep them visually separate from the RevenueCat invoice plans, because the two are easy to confuse.

### 5.10 PDF and sharing
- The PDF is built on the phone from HTML, with page numbers added afterwards.
- **Templates:** Classic (serif type, black borders), Modern (blue header band, blue totals) and Compact (denser). The default is chosen in settings. The template can be switched on the preview screen, but that choice is not saved to the invoice.
- **PDF content, top to bottom:**
  - Logo (at most 48 × 140 px), business name, address, phone, email, website and Tax ID.
  - "Invoice", the invoice number and a payment-status badge.
  - **Bill to:** name, address, phone, email, website.
  - Invoice date and due date.
  - Line-item table.
  - Totals: Subtotal, Discount, Tax, Total, Paid, and Remaining or Overpaid.
  - Payment history table: date, method, reference, amount.
  - Notes and Terms.
- **Table columns follow the invoice's method:** Item (with description underneath), SKU, Qty, Unit, Weight, Length, Width, Height, Unit price, Discount, Tax, Total. Only the method's fields appear. Columns are weighted so they always fit the page width; a Volume invoice is the densest case.
- **Constraints to solve:**
  - Body text is currently 7.5–9 px, which is too small. Propose a readable layout that still fits a portrait page with up to about 10 columns.
  - The page size isn't set explicitly. Specify safe areas for both A4 and US Letter.
  - Rows must not split across pages, and the table header must repeat on each page.
- **PDF Preview & Share screen:**
  - A header strip with status, customer and total.
  - A template selector.
  - An in-app preview. This is a native re-drawing, not the real PDF; say so in the UI.
  - **Quick share:**
    - **WhatsApp** opens a chat with a text message (number, total, remaining and an app link). It does **not** attach the PDF.
    - **Email PDF** attaches the PDF, addressed to the customer's email if known. It is hidden or disabled when no email app exists.
    - **Share Link** opens the system share sheet with the app link.
  - A sticky bar with **Share PDF** (system share sheet with the file) and **Preview PDF** (system print preview).
  - Loading, not-found and error states, plus a "Couldn't complete this action" alert for each failure.
- **Share links** are app deep links (`metriqo://invoice/<id>`, `metriqo://card/<slug>`). They only open on a phone that has Metriqo installed, and they are not web pages. Don't design them as "view online" links (see §11).

### 5.11 Digital business card
- **Fields** (one combined form with the business profile): business name, owner/contact name, logo, phone, email, website, address, currency, tax/VAT number, and social links (WhatsApp Business, Facebook Page, Instagram Handle, Google Maps Location).
- **Business Card screen:** a card preview (including a small QR code containing a vCard contact) and contact actions.

- **Share Card screen:** quick share via WhatsApp, Email, SMS, or More (system share); a "Share business card" button; and a link to the QR code.

## 7. Flows to design end-to-end (with every state)

1. **New invoice:** Dashboard → limit check (modal if the limit is reached) → Select Customer (or create one inline) → Invoice Items (choose method; add from catalog, filtered, or create an item inline; or add a custom line) → Line editor (both price modes; validation) → Review (dates, notes, totals) → Save → limit check again → Invoice Detail.

## 11. Known inconsistencies — design for current behaviour and flag each one in your notes

1. The Review screen says "DRAFT" and "Ready to issue", and Invoice Detail says "Issued Invoice", but there is no draft state. Saving issues the invoice.
2. The step indicator reads "Step 2 of 3 · Next: Payment & Review" on Items and "Step 3 of 3" on Review, but step 1 (Select Customer) shows no indicator.
3. **Time method:** the time-unit dropdown is not shown in the line editor (it's inside a card that only appears for weight or length fields), and catalog items have no time unit. Time lines therefore always use "hour". Design the time-unit selector as intended; developers must fix this.
4. **Custom method:** weight, length, width and height can be turned on, but the price is still quantity × price; those values are only informational. Also, the chosen custom field set isn't saved with each invoice, so changing it later changes which columns old Custom invoices show on the PDF.
5. The default tax rate in Invoice Settings is saved but not applied anywhere.
6. Create Customer has structured address fields, but Edit Customer has a single address field (plus placeholder City/Postcode/Country). Customer data stores one address string.
7. The QR Code screen encodes a `metriqo://` app link, which a customer's camera can't use without the app. The small QR on the card preview encodes a vCard, which works anywhere. Invoice and card links are app-only links that the app doesn't currently route to a screen.
8. Deleting a customer who has invoices fails with a generic "Please try again." message. The real reason is that their invoices must be deleted first.
9. The invoice delete confirmation doesn't mention that payments are deleted too.
10. "VAT" vs "Tax" wording is mixed. "Biometric Unlock (Face ID)" uses iOS wording on an Android app.
11. The Account screen offers both "Plans & subscription" (invoice plans) and "Upgrade to Plus" (cloud storage, "Coming soon"). These are easy to confuse.
12. "Email PDF" is shown with a placeholder label when no email app exists, instead of a clear "No email app" state.
13. Bottom bars and sticky footers don't add the bottom safe-area inset. On Android gesture or 3-button navigation they may sit under the system bar (needs screenshot confirmation).

## 12. Accessibility and Android requirements

- WCAG 2.2 AA contrast: 4.5:1 for body text and 3:1 for large text and icons. Never use colour alone for status; always pair it with a text label.
- Touch targets of at least 48 × 48 dp, with 8 dp spacing between them.
- Support font scaling up to 200%: no clipped money values, and long names wrap or truncate with ellipsis on purpose.
- Respect Android safe areas: status bar, display cutouts, the gesture bar, and 3-button navigation. Apply bottom insets to tab bars, sticky docks and modals. Handle the keyboard on forms (sticky actions must stay visible above it).
- Money: right-aligned tabular figures, the business currency symbol, two decimals, negative discounts shown as "-£x".
- Screen reader labels for icon-only buttons, a selected state on chips and toggles, and announced errors.
- Portrait phone sizes: design at 360 × 800 dp and check 412 × 915 dp.
